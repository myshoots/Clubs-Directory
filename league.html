League rules

  • Free to enter. There are no prizes, stakes, entry fees or betting.
  • Clubs are ranked by the total of their members' round scores in the period.
  • The number of members who shot is shown beside each total.
  • A club is ranked once at least three of its members have shot in the period.
  • Periods are this week, this month and the season. The season runs from 1 January to 31 December.
  • At the end of each season, the club at the top of the season table is named champion.
  • All scores are self-reported by members and are not verified or certified (until v2).
  • Members choose the club a round counts toward.
  • Open to adults aged 18 and over.
  • Club league tables show club names, members who shot and totals only. No individual shooter names are shown.
  • We may remove a club or a score that breaks these rules.
