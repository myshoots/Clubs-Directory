League rules

  • Free to enter. There are no prizes, stakes or entry fees.
  • All scores are self-reported by members and are not verified or certified. Verification comes with MyShoots® V2.
  • Members choose the club a round counts toward.
  • Open to adults aged 18 and over.
  • Club league tables show club names and combined totals only. No individual shooter names are shown.
  • We may remove a club or a score that breaks these rules.
