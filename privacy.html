MyShoots® Privacy and Cookie Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

MySports Community® Ltd, trading as MyShoots®, respects your privacy. This Policy explains what personal data we collect when you use the MyShoots® websites, why we collect it, and how we use and protect it. It also sets out your rights, and how cookies are used on the Platform. Please read it carefully.

1. Who We Are

1.1

We are MySports Community® Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales under company number 15325915, trading as MyShoots®. Our registered office is 128 City Road, London EC1V 2NX.

1.2

We are the “controller” of the personal data described in this Policy. This means we decide how and why your personal data is used. We are registered with the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) under registration number ZB654638.

1.3

For anything to do with your personal data, please contact us by email at info@myshoots.io, by telephone on +44 (0)7748 187330, or by post at our registered office address.

2. What This Policy Covers

2.1

This Policy applies to our website at www.myshoots.io, all of its subdomains (including clubs.myshoots.io, calendar.myshoots.io, brands.myshoots.io and partners.myshoots.io), and the ScoreCard and Field Card tools (together, the “Platform”). It also applies to our dealings with clubs, shooting grounds, brands and partners.

2.2

The Platform contains links to other websites, including those of clubs, brands and partners. We are not responsible for how those websites use personal data, so please read their privacy policies.

3. The Law

3.1

We use personal data in line with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR), the Data Protection Act 2018 and the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations 2003 (together, “Data Protection Law”).

3.2

Personal data means any information about you from which you can be identified, directly or indirectly. It includes obvious information such as your name and contact details, and less obvious information such as your IP address and other online identifiers.

4. The Personal Data We Collect

4.1

Visitors to the Platform. When you visit the Platform, our hosting providers automatically collect technical information such as your IP address, browser type, device type, operating system and the pages you visit. On www.myshoots.io, cookies are also used (see Part 12).

4.2

Enquiries. If you contact us, we collect your name, contact details and anything else you choose to tell us.

4.3

Clubs and shooting grounds. If you list a club or ground, or submit events, we collect the name, role, email address and telephone number of the contact person. We also collect the club’s details, including its address and postcode, website, social media links, logo, images, videos and event details. Club contact details that you choose to publish appear publicly in the club directory and events calendar.

4.4

Brands and partners. If you list a brand or become a partner, we collect the name, role and contact details of the people we deal with, and the content you supply for your listing.

4.5

Account holders. Where a feature of the Platform needs an Account, we collect your name, email address, password (stored securely in encrypted form), profile information, and any photographs, posts and comments you share.

4.6

ScoreCard and Field Card. If you use these tools and choose to save or send your records to us (for example, so they can be carried into MyShoots® Pro), we collect the records you enter. These include dates, the ground or shoot, the discipline and your scores or records. We also collect the name and email address you give us.

4.7

MyShoots® Pro founding list. If you join the founding list, we collect your name and email address. If you refer friends, we collect the names and email addresses you give us so that we can invite them. Please only refer people who are happy to hear from us.

4.8

Payments. If you buy a Paid Service, payment is processed by Stripe. We receive confirmation of payment, your billing name and address, and the last four digits and expiry date of your card. We never see or store your full card number.

4.9

Marketing. If you sign up to our newsletter or events digest, we collect your name, email address and your marketing preferences.

4.10

Special category data. We do not currently ask for health information, criminal offence data or any other special category data. If future features of MyShoots® Pro need information of this kind, we will update this Policy and ask for your explicit consent before collecting it.

5. How We Use Your Personal Data and Our Legal Basis

5.1

Data Protection Law requires us to have a lawful basis for each way we use your personal data. We use it as follows:

Running the Platform, keeping it secure, and fixing problems
Legitimate interests (operating a safe and working Platform).
Responding to your enquiries
Legitimate interests (responding to people who contact us) or, where your enquiry relates to a contract, taking steps at your request before entering into it.
Creating and managing your Account and providing the features that need one
Performance of our contract with you (our Terms of Use).
Publishing club, ground, event and brand listings
Performance of our contract with the club, brand or partner, and legitimate interests (providing a useful directory and calendar for shooters).
Saving your ScoreCard and Field Card records
Consent, which you give when you choose to save or send your records.
Selling and providing Paid Services, taking payment and sending service messages
Performance of our contract with you.
Keeping accounting and tax records
Legal obligation.
Running the MyShoots® Pro founding list and contacting people you refer
Consent (yours, and that of the people you refer when they choose to join).
Sending marketing emails, such as our events digest
Consent. You can withdraw your consent at any time using the unsubscribe link in any email.
Contacting clubs, brands and partners about our services
Legitimate interests (developing our business). You can object at any time.
Enforcing our terms, handling reports, and dealing with legal claims
Legitimate interests (protecting Users and our business) and, where required, legal obligation.
5.2

We do not use your personal data for automated decision-making that has legal or similarly significant effects on you.

5.3

We do not sell your personal data.

Wix.com

GitHub (Microsoft)

6.2

We may also share personal data with:

  • developers and contractors who build and maintain the Platform, under confidentiality obligations;
  • our professional advisers, such as our accountant and lawyers;
  • the police, regulators or other authorities, where the law requires it or where it is necessary to protect the safety of any person; and
  • a buyer of, or investor in, our business, if we sell, transfer or merge all or part of it. In that case, they may use your personal data in the same ways as described in this Policy.
6.3

Some information is public by design. Club listings, events and brand listings are visible to everyone. For any features that need an Account, what other Users can see depends on the features you use and your settings.

7. International Transfers

7.1

Some of our service providers, such as Wix, GitHub, Google, Airtable, Cloudinary and Stripe, may process personal data outside the UK, including in the United States.

7.2

Where this happens, we make sure your personal data is protected. We rely either on UK adequacy regulations (including the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, where the provider is certified) or on the International Data Transfer Agreement or Addendum approved by the Information Commissioner.

8. How Long We Keep Your Personal Data

8.1

We keep personal data only for as long as we need it for the purposes described in this Policy:

Account data
while your Account is open. When you delete your Account, we delete your personal data within 30 days, except where we need to keep it for the reasons below.
Enquiries
up to 2 years after the enquiry is resolved.
Club, ground, brand and partner records
while the listing or relationship continues, and up to 2 years afterwards.
Event submissions
events leave the public calendar once they have finished. We keep submission records for up to 2 years.
ScoreCard and Field Card records you save with us
until you ask us to delete them, or until they are carried into your MyShoots® Pro account.
Founding list and marketing
until you unsubscribe or withdraw your consent.
Payment and accounting records
6 years after the end of the financial year to which they relate, as required by law.
Reports and enforcement records
as long as reasonably necessary to keep the Platform safe and to deal with any legal claims.

9. How We Protect Your Personal Data

9.1

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data. These include encrypted connections (HTTPS), secure password storage, access controls limited to those who need access, and choosing service providers with strong security standards.

9.2

No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure. If we become aware of a personal data breach that is likely to put you at risk, we will tell you and the Information Commissioner’s Office as required by law.

10. Your Rights

10.1

Under Data Protection Law, you have the right to:

  • be informed about how we use your personal data (this Policy);
  • access the personal data we hold about you (a “subject access request”);
  • have inaccurate or incomplete personal data corrected;
  • have your personal data deleted;
  • restrict how we use your personal data;
  • object to our use of your personal data where we rely on legitimate interests, and to direct marketing at any time;
  • withdraw your consent at any time, where we rely on consent (this does not affect anything we did before you withdrew it); and
  • data portability, meaning you can receive a copy of personal data you gave us in a commonly used format, where we use it on the basis of consent or contract and by automated means.
10.2

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details in Part 1.3. There is normally no charge. We may need to confirm your identity before we act.

10.3

We will respond within one month of receiving your request. If your request is complex, we may extend this by up to two further months, and we will tell you if we need to.

11. Children

11.1

The Platform is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. Shooters aged 13 to 17 may only have an Account with the consent of a parent or guardian.

11.2

If you believe we hold personal data about a child under 13, please contact us and we will delete it.

12. Cookies

12.1

Cookies are small files placed on your device when you visit a website. Our main website, www.myshoots.io, is built on Wix. Wix places essential cookies that are needed for the website to work and to keep it secure. Other cookies, such as analytics cookies, are only used if you agree to them through the cookie banner. You can change your choices at any time using the cookie settings link on the website, or through your browser settings.

12.2

Our subdomains (clubs.myshoots.io, calendar.myshoots.io, brands.myshoots.io and partners.myshoots.io), the ScoreCard and Field Card do not use analytics or advertising cookies. The ScoreCard and Field Card may store your entries on your own device (using your browser’s local storage) so that they are not lost while you use them. This information stays on your device unless you choose to send it to us.

12.3

Embedded content and links from other websites (for example, maps, videos or brand websites) may set their own cookies. These are covered by those websites’ own policies.

13. Complaints

13.1

If you are unhappy with how we have used your personal data, please contact us first using the details in Part 1.3. We will acknowledge your complaint within 30 days and investigate it without undue delay.

13.2

You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO), the UK’s data protection regulator, at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

14. Changes to this Policy

14.1

We may update this Policy from time to time, for example if the law changes or if we change our services in a way that affects how we use personal data. The date at the top shows when it was last updated. If we make significant changes, we will tell you on the Platform and, if you have an Account, by email.
