MyShoots® Acceptable Use Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Please read this Acceptable Use Policy carefully before using the Platform. It sets out the standards that apply when you use the MyShoots® websites. These standards apply when you submit Content, communicate with other Users, or otherwise interact with the Platform. By using the Platform you accept this Policy. If you do not agree to it, you must stop using the Platform.

This Policy forms part of our Terms of Use (clubs.myshoots.io/terms). Words defined in our Terms of Use have the same meanings in this Policy.

1.2

2. Who This Policy Applies To

2.1

This Policy applies to everyone who uses the Platform. That includes shooters, clubs and shooting grounds, event organisers, brands, partners and visitors. It covers all parts of the Platform, including www.myshoots.io and its subdomains, and the ScoreCard and Field Card.

3. Changes to this Policy

3.1

We may change this Policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last updated. If we make significant changes, we will highlight them on the Platform and, if you have an Account, tell you by email.

3.2

If any part of the current version of this Policy conflicts with a previous version, the current version applies unless we say otherwise.

4. Acceptable Use of the Platform

4.1

You may only use the Platform lawfully. In particular:

  • you must comply with all laws and regulations that apply to you;
  • you must not use the Platform in any way, or for any purpose, that is unlawful or fraudulent;
  • you must not knowingly send, upload or transmit any virus, malware or other code designed to harm any hardware, software or data;
  • you must not send unsolicited or unauthorised advertising or marketing material (“spam”);
  • you must not use the Platform to bully, threaten, harass, intimidate, insult or humiliate anyone;
  • you must not use the Platform to harm, or attempt to harm, children in any way;
  • you must not scrape, harvest or systematically collect Content or other Users’ details from the Platform;
  • you must not create false Accounts, false club listings or false events, or submit scores or records that you know to be false in a way intended to mislead others; and
  • you must comply with the Content Standards in Part 6.

5. Interactive Services

5.1

The Platform includes the following interactive services:

  • club and shooting ground listings, and the forms used to create and update them;
  • event submissions for the events calendar;
  • any community features, such as profiles, posts, comments and photograph uploads, where we make them available;
  • the ScoreCard and Field Card tools; and
  • contact forms and email.
5.2

We assess the risks that our interactive services may pose to Users, including children, and decide what moderation is appropriate.

5.3

Club listings and calendar events are reviewed by a person before they are published. Other User Content, such as community posts and comments, is not checked before it appears. We review it when it is reported to us or when we become aware of a problem.

5.4

Children aged 13 to 17 may only use the interactive services with the consent and supervision of a parent or guardian. Children under 13 may not use them.

6. Content Standards

6.1

These standards apply to all User Content and all communications made using the Platform. You must not submit, share or communicate anything that:

  • is sexually explicit;
  • in any way sexualises children (including child sexual abuse material);
  • is obscene, deliberately offensive, hateful or inflammatory;
  • promotes violence, or threatens violence against any person;
  • promotes, encourages or supports terrorism;
  • promotes or assists any unlawful activity, including poaching, the unlawful killing or taking of wildlife, or the unlawful possession, sale or transfer of firearms, ammunition or prohibited weapons;
  • shows or encourages unsafe handling of firearms;
  • shows gratuitously graphic or disrespectful images of animals or game;
  • offers firearms, shotguns, ammunition or other items requiring a certificate or licence for sale or transfer (the Platform is not a marketplace for these items);
  • discloses another person’s firearm or shotgun certificate details, home address or security arrangements;
  • is defamatory of any person;
  • bullies, insults, intimidates or humiliates any person;
  • discriminates against any person or group on the grounds of race, nationality, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, religion or belief, disability or age;
  • is intended, or likely, to threaten, harass, alarm, upset or embarrass another person;
  • is intended, or likely, to deceive;
  • infringes anyone’s privacy, or uses their personal data in a way you have no right to;
  • impersonates any person, or misrepresents your identity or affiliation (including falsely claiming to represent a club, ground, brand or governing body), in a way that is calculated to deceive (obvious parodies are allowed, provided they meet the other standards in this Part 6);
  • implies an affiliation with us or anyone else where there is none;
  • infringes anyone’s intellectual property rights, including copyright, designs, patents, trade marks and database rights; or
  • breaches any legal duty owed to another person, including contractual duties and duties of confidence.
6.2

Whenever you submit Content or communicate using the Platform, you must make sure that it:

  • is truthful and accurate where it states facts (including event dates, prices, locations and club details);
  • states only opinions that you genuinely hold; and
  • complies with all applicable laws and regulations.
6.3

If you are a club, brand or partner, any advertising or promotional Content you submit must also comply with the UK Code of Non-broadcast Advertising and Direct & Promotional Marketing (the CAP Code).

7. Reporting Content

7.1

If you see Content that you believe is illegal or breaches this Policy, please report it by emailing info@myshoots.io with the subject line “Report”, or by using any reporting tool we provide on the Platform. Please tell us where the Content is and why you are concerned.

7.2

We will acknowledge reports and review them promptly. We will remove illegal content swiftly once we become aware of it, and we will prioritise reports involving risk to children or threats of violence.

7.3

We may report illegal content, and the details of the User who posted it, to the police or other relevant authorities.

7.4

We will let you know the outcome of your report where we can.

8. Breaches of this Policy

8.1

A breach of this Policy is also a material breach of our Terms of Use. If you breach this Policy, we may take one or more of the following actions:

  • remove, temporarily or permanently, your User Content or other submission;
  • issue you with a written warning;
  • suspend or terminate your Account or your right to use the Platform;
  • remove or suspend a club, brand or partner listing (in which case our Terms of Sale apply to any Paid Services);
  • take legal proceedings against you to recover our costs (on an indemnity basis) resulting from your breach;
  • take further legal action against you;
  • disclose information to law enforcement authorities where required, or where we reasonably consider it necessary; and/or
  • take any other action we reasonably consider appropriate and lawful.
8.2

If we take action against your Content or Account, we will normally tell you why, unless the law or a safety concern prevents us.

8.3

If you think we have made the wrong decision, you can ask us to review it by emailing info@myshoots.io within 28 days, explaining why. We will reconsider the decision and tell you the outcome.

8.4

To the extent permitted by law, we exclude all liability for actions we take in good faith under this Part 8 in response to a breach of this Policy.
